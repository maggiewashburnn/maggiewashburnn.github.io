ABOUT MERCH ZINES

INSCAPE MAGAZINE

 

INSCAPE ZINE was an idea born in 2023 by Maggie Washburn, and put in action in 2024 by Co Founders Duncan Carr and Maggie Washburn. INSCAPE volumes feature Charleston student artists of all mediums and backgrounds, in hopes of sharing as many stories and amplifying as many voices as possible. It is truly a treat to work on these projects.


The first two zines were produced and published by Duncan Carr and Maggie Washburn. Issue Three is in the works, completely produced and published by women. Bella Wallen has taken Duncan Carr’s leadership position, and she and Maggie have been working tirelessly to create the most meaningful zine yet.


Below you will find merchandise designed by Maggie Washburn, all proceeds for each piece will be donated to various women’s charities - we highly encourage you to order! You will also find links to previous INSCAPE zines, this is a piece of our history and we are proud of both issues. That being said, we have learned a lot since publishing our first zine, and we hope our growth is evident. Feel free to follow us on instagram @inscapezine and reach out via DM with any questions!
